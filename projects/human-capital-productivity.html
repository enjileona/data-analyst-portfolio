01 / PROJECT OVERVIEW

What problem does
this project solve?

A concise explanation of the business question and the scope of your analysis.

Workforce size alone does not explain organizational productivity. This project explores how employee and financial metrics can be brought together to help management compare productivity across business units and identify areas for further investigation.
