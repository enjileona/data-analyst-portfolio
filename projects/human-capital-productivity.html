01 / PROJECT OVERVIEW

The business question.
A data-led perspective.

The business question and analytical scope behind this project.

Workforce size alone does not explain organizational productivity. This project explores how employee and financial metrics can be brought together to help management compare productivity across business units and identify areas for further investigation.

How can workforce productivity be evaluated alongside business performance?
Scope1,000+ employees
FocusWorkforce productivity
ToolsExcel · Tableau
DeliverableInteractive dashboard

02 / ANALYTICAL FOCUS

Three perspectives.
One clearer picture.

Three dimensions for exploring workforce productivity. Specific conclusions should be validated against the dashboard data.

INSIGHT 01

Productivity by business unit

Compare output per employee across units to identify differences worth investigating.

Analysis question: Which unit stands out, and why?
INSIGHT 02

Workforce cost efficiency

Explore the relationship between employee-related costs and business output.

Analysis question: What does the cost-to-output comparison reveal?
INSIGHT 03

Regional performance patterns

Identify regional variation and potential drivers that merit deeper analysis.

Analysis question: What pattern appears across regions?
